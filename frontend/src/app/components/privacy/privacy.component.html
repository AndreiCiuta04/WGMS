WGMS demo

Privacy and Data Usage

Project scope

WGMS is a university demo project and not a production service or legal privacy policy.

Data we store

WGMS stores account data such as name, email, and login identity; household/WG membership data; tasks and cleaning schedules; expenses and payments; shopping list items and requests; dashboard/history data derived from app activity; and technical/security data needed for login and session handling.

Why we store it

Data is stored to provide authentication, household membership, task assignment, cleaning schedules, expense/payment tracking, shopping aggregation, and dashboard/history views.

Data minimization

The app should only expose data needed for the current feature. Password hashes and tokens are not shown to users or included in exports.

User rights supported in this demo

Users can view their own profile/account data through GET /api/v1/users/me, export their data through GET /api/v1/users/me/export, and create/update/delete domain data where the app implements those actions.

Current limitations

A full production GDPR process is not implemented. Formal retention rules are not implemented. Account deletion is implemented as anonymization so historical task, payment, and expense records remain consistent. This page is not a legal privacy policy for production deployment.

Contact / project note

For this university project, GDPR-related limitations are documented in the QA/NFR checklist.
