Privacy, Terms, and GDPR Demo Support

Privacy and Data Usage

Project scope

WGMS is a university demo project and not a production service or legal privacy policy. It is built to demonstrate shared-flat management features and reasonable GDPR support for project scope.

Data we store

WGMS stores account data such as name, email, password hash, and user role; WG membership data such as household membership, invitation links, roles, and join/leave timestamps where implemented; tasks, assigned chores, completion status, due dates, and task history; cleaning schedules with title, recurrence, assignment, and due dates; expenses, splits, payments, and balances; shopping items, visibility, status, and shopping history; dashboard/history data derived from app activity; and technical/security data needed for login, sessions, timestamps, errors, and authentication handling.

No special-category personal data is required for WGMS. The app records expense/payment tracking data for household organization, but it does not process real banking data, payment-provider data, or real money transfers.

Why we store it

Data is stored to provide authentication, WG membership management, task assignment, cleaning schedules, expense/payment tracking, shopping management, and dashboard/history display.

Data minimization

The app should only expose data needed for the current feature. Password hashes and tokens are not shown to users and are not included in exports.

Terms and Conditions

WGMS is for university/demo use. Users should enter only appropriate demo/project data and are responsible for the correctness of the tasks, expenses, payments, and shopping items they create.

Data entered into a household may be visible to authorized members of the same WG where a feature requires sharing. WGMS does not perform real money transfers; in short, there are no real money transfers. It is not a legal, accounting, tax, rental-management, or banking system.

Terms version: v1.0.

GDPR Demo Support

Users can view their own profile/account data through GET /api/v1/users/me and export their data through GET /api/v1/users/me/export. Domain data can be corrected or deleted where the app implements editing/deletion features. Account deletion is implemented as anonymization so historical task, payment, and expense records remain consistent.

Current Limitations

WGMS does not claim full production GDPR compliance. Formal retention rules are not implemented. Account deletion/anonymization is limited to preserving historical household consistency. No real banking/payment data is processed, no special-category personal data is required, and external hosting/processor handling is only relevant for a real deployment.
